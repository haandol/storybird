# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Be respectful. Assume good faith, discuss the code rather than the person, and
protect the private content Storybird can capture.

## Expected Behavior

- Keep technical disagreement specific and constructive.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes for scope, privacy, or platform
  reasons.
- Use synthetic screenshots and demos in public discussions.
- Remove credentials, customer names, messages, and private dashboards from
  reproduction material.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption,
publishing another person's private information, or sharing captured content
without permission are not acceptable in any project space.

## Reporting

Report sensitive conduct or security concerns privately through
`https://github.com/haandol/storybird/security/advisories/new`. Public,
non-sensitive project concerns may be raised in an issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close pull requests, or block accounts
when needed to enforce this policy.
